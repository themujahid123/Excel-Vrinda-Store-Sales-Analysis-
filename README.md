##  Insights

- **Customer Gender Analysis:** Women are more likely to buy compared to men (~65%).  
- **Top Contributing States:** Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh are the top 3 states contributing to sales.  
- **Age Group Contribution:** Adult age group (30–49 yrs) is contributing the most (~50%).  
- **Top Sales Channels:** Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra are the channels contributing maximum sales.  

### Final Conclusion / Recommendation
To improve Vrinda store sales:

- Target **women customers** in the **30–49 yrs** age group.  
- Focus on customers living in **Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh**.  
- Promote products via **Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra** using **ads, offers, and coupons** to increase engagement and conversions.
